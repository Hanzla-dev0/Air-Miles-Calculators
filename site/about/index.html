About Air Miles Calculators

A fast, free way to measure the distance between airports.

What we do

Air Miles Calculators provides a simple air miles calculator. Choose two airports and see the great-circle distance in air miles, kilometres and nautical miles, with an estimated flight time. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no fees.

How the numbers are produced

Distances come from the haversine formula applied to airport coordinates. Read the full explanation in our guide to how air miles are calculated.

Accuracy

Results are great-circle estimates for planning and information. Real flights follow airways and weather, so the distance flown is usually slightly longer. We cannot guarantee that airport data is free from error; if you spot one, let us know.
